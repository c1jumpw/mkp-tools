Help & guide

Say it once. It lands in the right list.

Dispatch It sends quick captures into ClickUp. Type or dictate a thought and smart routing picks the list, tidies the title, reads dates and checks for duplicates. If you prefer, you can pick the business and list yourself. This guide shows every screen and setting with real screenshots. The numbers on each picture match the list beside it.

10

Offline & drafts

Everyday
  • No connection? Manual captures are saved with their files and sent automatically when you're back. Home shows "N captures waiting to send" with Retry now.
  • Not sure it went through? "ClickUp didn't confirm this one" means it's saved. Before resending, the app checks whether it already arrived, so you won't get a copy.
  • Drafts. Half-typed captures are kept for 7 days. Reopening shows "Draft restored." with Discard.
  • Old version showing? Tap the refresh icon on Home.

A3

Auto-filing & confidence

Admin

Every suggestion gets a confidence from 0 to 100%. A capture files itself only when auto-filing is on, it scores at or above the threshold, and no likely duplicate was found. Otherwise the person sees the Confirm screen.

Low Medium High 75 85 92
0%50%100%

75 Bold85 Balanced (default)92 Cautious

  • Cautious files less on its own; people confirm more.
  • Balanced (85%) is the default.
  • Bold files more on its own; check Activity more often.

Activity's summary shows how many suggestions were kept. If people often change the list or undo auto-filed captures, move toward Cautious.

A5

Destinations

Admin
  • New ClickUp lists appear on their own under "More lists in this space" and in Other list…, after live updates or a sync.
  • To add or rename a capture type, edit mkp-dispatch/js/config.js (business › capture type › List ID) and publish the change to GitHub.
  • Find a List ID by opening the list in ClickUp: it's the number after /li/ in app.clickup.com/<workspace>/v/li/<LIST_ID>.
  • Configured lists are "yours". Smart routing prefers them and the list chooser tags them.

Passcode & secrets

Admin

Change the passcode

  1. Run wrangler secret put APP_PASSCODE from worker/ and enter the new one.
  2. Every device then sees "passcode was rejected". On each: Settings › Forget passcode on this device, then enter the new one.

A7

Limits & Undo rules

Admin
  • Attachments: 90 MB per file.
  • Gemini: limits depend on the key. When it's busy, smart capture says so and offers the manual picker with the text carried over.
  • Duplicate index: the free Cloudflare tier holds about 6,500 open tasks.
  • Undo won't delete a task with subtasks, one edited more than 10 minutes after filing, or one not created by that send. Records older than 180 days can't be undone.
  • Worker CPU: if Settings shows "Exceeded CPU" sync errors, the $5/month Workers Paid plan removes the limit.

A9

Keeping help current

Admin

This guide and the getting-started tour rebuild themselves. You only write the What's new note.

  1. Push an update to the Dispatch It folder on GitHub as usual.
  2. An automatic job (GitHub › Actions › "Refresh guides and tours") opens the app with example data, retakes every screenshot, rebuilds this guide and the tour, and saves them back to GitHub. It takes a few minutes; then the live app shows the new pictures.
  3. Say what changed by adding an entry to the top of mkp-dispatch/CHANGELOG.md: a ## date · title line and a few - lines. Everyone sees it once, as What's new, the next time they open the app.
If the job failsGitHub emails you when a numbered marker can't find its button, which means a screen changed. The log lists which picture and marker. Update the screenshot steps in guides/ and the matching text, then push again. Until then the guide keeps its previous pictures.

Everything the job uses lives in the guides/ folder: the screenshot steps, the guide text (guide.src.html), the tour slides (tour.json) and a README.
